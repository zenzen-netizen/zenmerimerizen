  extrem ke-capture rapat (mendekati true peak/trough), **resolusi bagus**.

**Yang TIDAK ada (pembatas faithful):**

  (PnL hasil = APPROX: asumsi exit ≈ L, abaikan overshoot/slippage.)

## Q4 — REKOMENDASI metode per hipotesis

| Hipotesis | Metode utama | Faithfulness | n tersedia | Prasyarat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **stopLossPct** | **BACKTEST** (lalu paper opsional) | near-FAITHFUL (trigger via `trough_pnl_pct ≤ L`) | **73/73** | tak ada — field lengkap; script offline baca lessons.json |
| **trailingTrigger / trailingDrop** | **BACKTEST utk RANKING → paper konfirmasi** | APPROX (urutan peak/trough tak diketahui) | peak/exit 73/73; trailing nyata 19 | backtest: field ada; paper: instance bersih |
| **screening-rug** | **FORWARD paper** (backtest = lemah) | backtest BURUK (cuma pool yg dimasuki, no skip-counterfactual) | sinyal entry 73/73 (terkonfound) | paper instance + `counterfactualReview`/candidate-memory utk lacak pool di-skip |
| **bin-width (bins_below / range)** | **BACKTEST coverage → paper utk fee-yield** | APPROX (excursion eksak, tapi fee/konsentrasi tak ter-replay) | price excursion **55/73** | backtest: `price_peak/trough_pct`+`bin_range`; paper utk interaksi fee |

**Ringkas keputusan:**
1. **stopLoss & trailing & bin-width** → mulai **BACKTEST offline** (gratis, instan, market-matched ke
   trade nyata kita). Cukup script baca `lessons.json` — TAK perlu instance/LLM. stopLoss paling kuat
   (faithful); trailing & bin-width = ranking kasar lalu **konfirmasi** kandidat teratas via paper pendek.
2. **screening-rug** → **FORWARD paper** (atau analisis counterfactual candidate-memory). Backtest mentah
   bias: tak punya pool yg kita TOLAK. Butuh horizon market berjalan + instance.
3. **Paper bench** baru perlu di-spin saat masuk tahap KONFIRMASI / screening-rug. Spin = clone folder +
   `dryRun:true`+`paperTrading:true` + keypair (tanpa dana) + RPC + (idealnya) model murah. ⚠️ ingat
   ⚠️ akurasi paper masih optimistik: fee ~tepat pasca-fix tapi gas=0 & slippage parsial — lihat
   `notes/paper-recon.md` Q2/Q6 (paper = bench MEKANIK, bukan forecast profit absolut).
